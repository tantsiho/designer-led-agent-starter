# 01 RAW DESIGN

Put the raw design draft here.

It can be long, messy, repetitive, and unlike a formal PRD. Do not rush to turn it into engineering specs.

It can include:

- product ideas
- user stories
- what you do not want the product to become
- rules
- exceptions
- places where you worry AI may misunderstand
- future ideas
- reference products
- drafts, chat logs, notes
